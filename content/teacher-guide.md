## Teacher guide

OpenSoma lets students **poke a virtual cell** built from real lab experiments. It works in any modern browser (laptop, Chromebook, tablet) with no login and no install.

## Suggested 45-minute lesson

- **5 min:** Warm-up question: "Why don't your cells grow out of control?"
- **10 min:** Guided tour **What is cancer?** (whole class on a projector, or in pairs).
- **15 min:** Pairs explore the **Playground**: turn off a gene in the cancer cell and compare it with the healthy-like cell. Ask each pair to find one gene that changes the cancer cell but not the healthy one.
- **10 min:** Mission **Do no harm**. Compare scores and strategies.
- **5 min:** Exit ticket: the lesson quiz, or the printable worksheet.

## Reading levels

Use the reading-level switch (top right). **Simple** needs no biology background; **Student** fits high-school biology; **Deep** fits undergraduates. The share links below set the level automatically.

## Talking points for honesty

- **● Measured vs. ◐ Predicted:** ask students why a scientist would want to know the difference.
- **Lab cells aren't people:** these are cell lines grown in dishes for decades. Most drugs that work on cells fail in people.
- **Not medical advice:** some students may have family members with cancer. The app never says whether a real person should take a drug. Point families to the National Cancer Institute (cancer.gov, 1-800-4-CANCER).

## Printable worksheets

Each lesson has a printable worksheet with a step-by-step note-taking sheet, the quiz, and an answer key (hidden when printed).
